WHY PUERTO RICO

THE NINE-DAY WEEK

Puerto Rico is the only place on earth with a nine-day week. Be in New York, in your office or anywhere you want, Monday through Thursday, from 9 or 10 am until 7 pm. You only need to be in Puerto Rico for about 30 weekends a year: thirty amazing weekends, in a great place, with great friends.

If you are in your late 40s and making real money, running a big advertising or management business, for example, the question is simple: wouldn't it be good to figure out how to run your business from Puerto Rico?
