# ORA World — call script, script checklist and 10 outreach variations

Built only from the owner's content brief (his recorded conversation, Nov 2026), the sales team's prospect email and
the TDM Brochure. Purpose (brief §8): every caller says the same thing, the way the owner wants it said; the system can
tell when a caller deviates; and the outreach has 10 variations of the same message.

**Before use (the brief's own note):** confirm the exact name of the tax law, the 4% change and the January 1 deadline
with ORA's tax team; verify the founder's credentials and the 65% figure; never promise a guaranteed health outcome.
Do not name the owner. Do not promise helicopter or airport pickups.

---

## 1. Call script (rehearsal-ready, ~8 minutes)

**Who we talk to (brief §7):** wealthy professionals and business owners, including doctors; around 49–50, the age
people start thinking about health and aging; people whose children are already taken care of.

### Opening (30 seconds)
> "Hi {{first_name}}, it's {{caller}} from ORA World. You asked about Tesoro Del Mar in Puerto Rico. Do you have
> five minutes? I'd like to understand what matters most to you."

Check the inquiry form's answer to **"What matters most to you?"** before the call and start there.

### 1 · The promise (brief §1)
> "ORA World is about two things: a longer life and a better life. Longevity is the foundation of our world. If you're
> going to live longer, you'll want to live better. And if you want a better life, you'll want to be stronger and
> fitter, and to feel it. ORA World makes all of that easy. Have you heard about anything like this? Never. That's
> ORA World."

### 2 · Preventive health (brief §2)
> "Planes don't fall from the sky. Redundancy and preventive maintenance. You have two lungs, two kidneys: you have
> redundancy. But you don't have preventive maintenance. Most people only see a doctor when something hurts, and by then
> something has often already broken. ORA checks you before something breaks, before you feel anything."

> "Let me ask you: what is the value of an extra 10 years for you? Would you pay a million? Five? Ten? You can't take
> anything with you, and your kids are already taken care of. Time and health become the real investment."

Do **not** say a longer life is guaranteed. Say: "If you follow what the doctors recommend, you give yourself the best
chance at a longer life."

### 3 · Fitness and nutrition (brief §3–§4)
> "If you already have a longer life, don't you want to have a better life? Some people look 50 or 60 at 80. Others
> already look old at 60. We help you be the first kind. The goal is the 95th to 98th percentile for your age. Your body
> is self-made: you can work on it in advance. At 50, the question is how to feel like 30 or 35."

What members get: a total body scan; a personal interview about their goals; nutritionists who set specifics like daily
protein targets; many meal options built around what they like; a ghost kitchen on the premises that cooks for them; a
fitness program designed to make getting fit easy; a connection to a list of cardiologists.

### 4 · The founder (brief §5)
> "ORA World was founded by a Harvard-trained heart surgeon. His clinics are within driving distance of about 65% of the
> US population, and longevity is one of his focus areas."

(Never say his name.)

### 5 · Why Puerto Rico (brief §6)
> "Puerto Rico is the only place on earth with a nine-day week. You can be in New York, in your office or anywhere you
> want, Monday through Thursday. You only need to be in Puerto Rico for about 30 weekends a year: thirty amazing
> weekends, in a great place, with great friends."

> "And the tax side runs under an official Puerto Rico law. You get a specific decree, approved by the government, that
> nobody can take away: 15 years plus a 15-year extension, about 30 years. Starting January 1st the rate is no longer
> zero; it will be 4%. You don't need to move now. You just need to apply. If your application is in by that date,
> you're grandfathered, even if the process finishes next year. We have a team of accountants to help; every situation
> is specific."

**Trust point:** "Everything is public. Google the law while we're on the call." (Official site: desarrollo.pr.gov.)

For a business owner in their late 40s:
> "Wouldn't it be good to figure out how to run your business from Puerto Rico?"

### 6 · Tesoro Del Mar (prospect email, brochure)
> "The first phase is Tesoro Del Mar in Dorado: 60 residences in Phase One, every unit with water views and oversized
> balconies, a nutritionist on staff, and private charter flights to your condo in Puerto Rico and back to the US.
> Early pre-construction pricing averages $2,250 per square foot: about $2,000 for the lower units and $2,500 for the
> higher ones. Later units will likely be sold at higher rates."

### 7 · Close
> "I'll send you the Phase 1 floor plans and the project presentation now. When can we schedule a follow-up call, or a
> visit to Dorado?"

Send the floor plans PDF and the presentation PDF (direct links, never the per-recipient tracked links), then book the
follow-up in the CRM.

## 4. CRM set-up notes (recommendations)

The website already sends each inquiry, including **"What matters most to you?"**, to the CRM webhook
(`CRM_WEBHOOK_URL`, see the README).

1. **One script, everywhere.** Load section 1 into the CRM as the call script, and section 3 as 10 email/SMS templates
   in one campaign, so every caller and every message says the same thing.
2. **Deviation check.** Turn on call recording and transcription, then score each transcript against the checklist in
   section 2 (an AI step can do this automatically): list missing points and any "never say" phrase, and flag the call
   for review.
3. **Workload.** Route leads by their "What matters most to you?" answer, so each caller opens with the topic the lead
   chose instead of a cold start; this protects callers with 100+ leads.
4. **AI that talks to leads (longer term).** The website's Ask ORA assistant already answers with the same wording
   (health, ghost kitchen, founder, nine-day week, decree, "do I need to move now"). The same question set can seed a
   CRM conversation bot, once the tax lines are confirmed.
